# Animation Principles

The tech-agnostic foundation for deciding how something should move. For motion designers and developers who want animation that feels natural instead of stiff, floaty, or robotic — covering easing curves, timing, the Graph Editor, and the 12 principles of animation.

## When it activates

- "Make this animation feel natural" or "my animation looks robotic / stiff / floaty / cheap"
- "Choose an easing curve" or "Easy Ease isn't enough" / "how do I use the Graph Editor"
- "Add overshoot or bounce", "make snappy motion", or "pick a duration for this transition"
- "Stagger a list animation", "sync animation to a beat", or "review motion for good timing"

## Example prompts

- "This dropdown feels cheap — fix the timing and easing so it reads as snappy."
- "Pick a duration and easing curve for a card flip, and stagger a 6-item list."
- "Review this motion for the 12 principles and tell me what's missing."
