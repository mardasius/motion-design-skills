# Motion Direction

The senior creative judgment that sits above specific techniques. For motion designers directing a piece — defining a motion language, setting tone and pacing, building motion hierarchy, and keeping everything consistent from shot to shot.

## When it activates

- "Direct a motion graphics video" or "do creative / art direction for a video"
- "Define a motion language" or "set the tone and pacing"
- "Make the animation feel consistent" or "decide what to animate (and what not to)"
- "Turn a brief / brand into motion choices"

## Example prompts

- "Direct this product video — define a motion language and set the tone and pacing."
- "Turn this brand brief into concrete motion choices and a hierarchy of what moves."
- "My sequence feels inconsistent shot to shot — give me direction to unify it."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — when to use it and the direction decision framework.
- `references/direction-playbook.md` — motion language, tone, pacing, hierarchy, and consistency playbook.
