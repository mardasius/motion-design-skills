# After Effects

Practical Adobe After Effects help for motion designers — expressions and rigging, export recipes, performance triage, and project hygiene. Covers everything from a wiggle expression to choosing a codec to figuring out why AE is slow or crashing.

## When it activates

- "Write an After Effects expression", "make a wiggle / loopOut / inertial bounce", or "rig a control with a slider / null"
- "Export with transparency / an alpha channel", "fix a black background on export", or "ProRes vs H.264 / which codec / what bitrate"
- "After Effects is slow / laggy / crashing", "RAM preview won't play in real time", or "how do I speed up After Effects"
- "Organize my After Effects project", "relink missing footage", "set a folder structure", or "write an ExtendScript / .jsx script"

## Example prompts

- "Write an inertial bounce expression and rig it to a slider control."
- "Export this comp with an alpha channel — my background keeps coming out black."
- "After Effects is crawling and RAM preview won't play in real time — help me speed it up."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — when to use it across expressions, export, performance, and hygiene.
- `references/export-recipes.md` — codec, bitrate, alpha, and Render Queue recipes.
- `references/expression-library.md` and `references/expressions.md` — ready-to-use expressions and rigging patterns.
- `references/performance-triage.md` — diagnosing slow, laggy, or crashing AE.
- `references/project-hygiene.md` — folder structure, naming, and relinking footage.
- `scripts/` — ExtendScript (.jsx) helpers.
