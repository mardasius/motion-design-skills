# Color Motion

Color for motion design — palette construction, gradients, perceptual interpolation, grading, and render color management. For designers and developers who want smooth color transitions, clean gradients, and previews that match the final render.

## When it activates

- "Build a color palette for a motion piece" or "color-grade a video for mood"
- "Make a gradient background", "animate a color transition", or "fix muddy / gray gradients"
- "Interpolate colors smoothly" or "convert colors to OKLCH"
- "Fix washed-out After Effects renders", "colors shifted after rendering", or "match preview to final video"

## Example prompts

- "Build a 5-color palette for a moody product reveal and animate the transition smoothly."
- "My gradient goes muddy in the middle — fix it with OKLCH interpolation."
- "My After Effects render looks washed out and shifted — make the export match my preview."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — when to use it plus palette, gradient, and grading workflow.
- `references/color-management.md` — render and video color management (sRGB, Rec.709, gamma).
- `references/palettes-and-interpolation.md` — palette construction and perceptual interpolation.
